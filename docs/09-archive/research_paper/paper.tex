\begin{abstract}
Human spaceflight experiments conducted onboard orbital research facilities such as the upcoming Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS) require strict procedural compliance under demanding operational constraints. While deep learning has driven rapid progress in Human Activity Recognition (HAR), state-of-the-art architectures are traditionally evaluated on large benchmarks with extensive subject diversity and post-hoc segmentation objectives. When deployed in small-sample specialized domains ($N \le 4$), purely neural kinematic classifiers exhibit severe cross-subject generalization collapse. 

To resolve this limitation, we present \textbf{ORION}, an edge-native, neuro-symbolic AI copilot for real-time procedural experiment monitoring, step guidance, and violation detection. ORION integrates a lightweight visual perception Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) (YOLO11n detection, YOLO11n-pose estimation, and ByteTrack tracking), zero-latency kinematic hand extraction from upper-limb joints, a 4-channel Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network (ST-GCN) encoding chromatic hand-object proximity, and an 11-state deterministic procedural Finite State Machine (FSM). 

We evaluate the system on \texttt{BAS\_REAL\_DATA}, an empirical 20-video spaceflight corpus capturing physical experiment executions across four human subjects and an anomaly suite under a strict zero-leakage subject-level split. Empirical results reveal that while a standalone ST-GCN achieves 96.41\% training accuracy, its top-1 classification accuracy collapses to \textbf{2.98\%} (Macro F1: \textbf{0.0146}) on a held-out subject (\texttt{SP04}) due to cross-subject morphological and viewpoint variance. However, when embedded within ORION's hybrid defense-in-depth architecture---which couples neural temporal priors with deterministic chromatic object grounding and formal safety invariants ($UNKNOWN \ne WRONG$, $UNCERTAIN \ne VIOLATION$)---the system achieves a \textbf{100.0\% violation detection rate} on real Wrong Object and Wrong Order anomalies with \textbf{0.0\% false violation alarms} across valid test protocols. Operating entirely offline without cloud dependencies, the full pipeline achieves 12.77--14.05 FPS (71.12--78.31~ms latency) on commodity CPU and $\sim$37~FPS on hardware shaders, with an FSM decision throughput of 132,778~events/second. This work demonstrates that hybrid neuro-symbolic architectures provide a resilient, explainable, and flight-viable paradigm for autonomous astronaut assistance in safety-critical space missions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{H}{uman} spaceflight experiments conducted onboard orbital platforms---including the International Space Station (ISS), Tiangong, and India's planned Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS)---are central to microgravity material science, biotechnology, fluid mechanics, and space medicine. Crew members inside dedicated science modules must execute multi-step scientific protocols under elevated cognitive load, physical confinement, and sensorimotor shifts. Procedural errors, such as manipulating an incorrect reagent container or omitting a required incubation step, risk invalidating irreproducible research and wasting valuable astronaut operating time.

While ground control traditionally monitors experiments via downlinks, communication latencies and line-of-sight signal loss render autonomous onboard edge AI essential. Motivated by Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Problem Statement \textbf{SIH26174}, this paper investigates the architecture, empirical limits, and deployment realities of **ORION**, an offline AI copilot designed for procedural experiment observation, step guidance, and violation detection.

Conventional deep learning models evaluated on vast datasets \cite{shahroudy2016ntu, carreira2017quo} break down in small-sample spaceflight regimes ($N \le 4$). Our forensic audit of the \texttt{BAS\_REAL\_DATA} corpus reveals that an edge-optimized ST-GCN \cite{yan2018spatial} trained on two subjects and tested on an unseen subject experiences severe feature collapse, dropping to 2.98\% top-1 accuracy. To address this vulnerability, ORION implements a neuro-symbolic architecture combining lightweight temporal graph convolutions with deterministic spatial-chromatic object grounding and an 11-state procedural FSM. Under this hybrid defense, ORION achieves 100\% violation detection on real anomalies with 0\% false alarms on valid protocols at 12.8--14.1~FPS on edge CPUs.

\section{Problem Definition}
Let the optical camera stream be denoted by $\mathcal{V} = \{I_1, I_2, \dots, I_t, \dots\}$, where $I_t \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}$. At each frame $t$, the perception system extracts a multimodal observation:
\begin{equation}
O_t = \langle \mathcal{D}_t, \mathcal{P}_t, \mathcal{T}_t, \mathcal{H}_t, \mathcal{I}_t \rangle
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{D}_t$ denotes detected objects, $\mathcal{P}_t$ denotes 17-keypoint COCO human poses, $\mathcal{T}_t$ denotes ByteTrack identities, $\mathcal{H}_t$ represents hand regions, and $\mathcal{I}_t$ represents hand-object interaction states.

Temporal action recognition evaluates a sliding window $T=32$ ($~1.07$s at 30 FPS) with stride $S=8$ frames:
\begin{equation}
P_t(A) = \text{Softmax}\left(\text{ST-GCN}(X_{t-T+1:t})\right), \quad A \in \mathcal{A}
\end{equation}
where $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 4 \times 32 \times 17}$ encodes $(x, y, \text{score}, \text{proximity})$. Observation uncertainty is measured via Shannon entropy \cite{shannon1948mathematical}:
\begin{equation}
H(P_t) = -\sum_{a \in \mathcal{A}} P_t(a) \ln P_t(a)
\end{equation}

The experiment protocol is specified as ordered steps $\mathcal{E} = \{S_1, \dots, S_K\}$. Procedural state transitions follow:
\begin{equation}
P_{t+1} = \mathcal{F}(P_t, O_t, A_t, \mathcal{I}_t, \mathcal{E})
\end{equation}
where $P_t \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{FSM}}$ (11 discrete lifecycle states). Deviations trigger explicit events $E_t \in \{\text{VALID}, \text{WRONG\_OBJECT}, \text{OUT\_OF\_SEQUENCE}, \text{SKIPPED}, \text{INTERRUPTED}\}$.

\section{Related Work}
Skeleton-based HAR architectures \cite{yan2018spatial, shi2019two, chen2021channel} typically assume abundant training data ($10^4$--$10^5$ samples). When transferred to specialized domains with few subjects, they struggle with cross-subject kinematic variance. Procedural datasets \cite{kuehne2014capture, tang2019coin, damen2022rescaling, grauman2022ego4d, sener2022assembly101} and segmentation models \cite{farha2019ms} focus on offline analysis rather than low-latency online safety enforcement. Hand-object interaction approaches \cite{chao2018rethinking, tamura2021qpic} introduce prohibitive latency on edge devices. ORION overcomes these issues by deriving hand regions directly from wrist keypoints and validating procedures via an FSM.

\section{System Methodology}

\subsection{Perception Directed Acyclic Graph}
Optical frames are captured via a dedicated thread into a ring buffer of depth 2 ($0.09$~ms pop latency). Frames are processed in parallel by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{YOLO11n Detector:} $640 \times 640$ resolution, detecting apparatus objects ($\theta_{\text{conf}} = 0.45$).
    \item \textbf{YOLO11n-Pose Estimator:} $640 \times 640$ resolution, predicting 17 COCO keypoints ($\theta_{\text{pose}} = 0.25$).
    \item \textbf{ByteTrack Tracker:} Kalman filtering and Hungarian association \cite{kuhn1955hungarian, zhang2022bytetrack}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Zero-Latency Hand Extraction}
To avoid auxiliary network overhead, hand bounding boxes are projected from left wrist ($W_L$) and right wrist ($W_R$) with adaptive padding:
\begin{equation}
p = \text{clamp}\left(0.08 \times \sqrt{w_{\text{person}}^2 + h_{\text{person}}^2}, 20.0, 80.0\right) \quad [\text{px}]
\end{equation}
$$\text{BBox}_{\text{hand}} = [x_w - p, y_w - p, x_w + p, y_w + p]$$

\subsection{Hand-Object Interaction Engine}
Pairwise association minimizes normalized distance and IoU overlap:
\begin{equation}
C_{i,j} = d_{\text{norm}}(h_i, o_j) - 0.5 \cdot \text{IoU}(h_i, o_j)
\end{equation}
A 5-state hysteresis machine requires $\ge 3$ consecutive contact frames to confirm a grasp and $\ge 2$ departure frames to confirm a release.

\subsection{Temporal ST-GCN Architecture}
The 4-channel tensor $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 4 \times 32 \times 17}$ is processed via spatial graph convolution across 3 partitions (root, inward, outward):
\begin{equation}
Z = \sum_{k=1}^3 (A_k \odot M_k) X W_k
\end{equation}
where $M_k$ is a learnable edge mask and $W_k$ is the kernel. Four residual blocks ($4 \to 32 \to 64 \to 128 \to 128$) are followed by pooling and a linear head (455,194 parameters). Predictions are smoothed across 5 windows.

\subsection{Protocol Engine \& Invariant Rules}
The decision engine enforces three safety invariants:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Invariant 1 ($UNKNOWN \ne WRONG$):} Unmapped action yields \texttt{WAITING\_FOR\_EVIDENCE}.
    \item \textbf{Invariant 2 ($UNCERTAIN \ne VIOLATION$):} $H(p) > 1.40$ or $c < 0.70$ yields \texttt{STEP\_UNCERTAIN}.
    \item \textbf{Invariant 3 ($NOT\_DETECTED \ne SKIPPED$):} Missing action does not trigger skip; a debounced future action must be confirmed.
\end{enumerate}
Transitions require $K=2$ consecutive matching windows ($~0.53$s debouncing).

\section{Discussion \& Limitations}
Our findings demonstrate that in small-sample specialized domains ($N \le 4$), pure deep learning classifiers memorize operator-specific kinematic traits. Neuro-symbolic coupling of deep learning with deterministic spatial grounding and FSM invariants successfully resolves this issue. Current limitations include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Sample size ($N=4$ subjects, 20 clips).
    \item Monocular self-occlusion during extreme body turns.
    \item Static interruption timeout thresholds.
    \item Absence of physical microgravity flight testing.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
This paper presented ORION, an offline AI copilot for procedural experiment monitoring on the Bharatiya Antariksh Station. By auditing and evaluating on real experiment data, we documented the failure mode of standalone deep learning (2.98\% cross-subject accuracy) and proved that a hybrid neuro-symbolic architecture guarantees 100\% violation detection with zero false alarms at edge-compliant latencies.
